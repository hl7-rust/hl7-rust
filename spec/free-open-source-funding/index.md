# Free open source funding

This project is free under all five of its licences whether or not anyone
pays anything, and stays that way. There is no paid tier and nothing that
unlocks with money — funding, where it exists, buys maintainer time and
nothing else.

## Status

- **GitHub Sponsors is live.** [`.github/FUNDING.yml`](../../.github/FUNDING.yml)
  points at <https://github.com/sponsors/joelparkerhenderson>, re-verified
  2026-08-28 as a real sponsor page rather than a "not accepting sponsors"
  state. [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)'s Money section and
  [`NEWS.md`](../../NEWS.md)'s Funding section both name it and are candid about what it
  buys — maintainer time, mostly dictionary coverage and answering issues
  — and what it does not: a support contract, a response-time guarantee,
  a roadmap commitment, or influence over what gets merged. Sponsorship is
  never a condition of having a bug fixed.
- **Open Collective is deliberately deferred, checked 2026-08-28.** This is
  the owner's decision, not a stalled blocker: creating a collective and
  choosing a fiscal host is an identity and financial step nobody should
  take but the owner, and it is not happening right now by choice. The
  deferral is informed rather than an excuse — no collective exists yet at
  any plausible slug (`hl7-rust`, `hl7rust`, `hl7-for-rust`, `hl7-2`,
  `er7-rust`, `joel-parker-henderson`, `joelparkerhenderson` all resolve to
  Open Collective's generic search shell, not a real collective).
  `.github/FUNDING.yml`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `NEWS.md` say so plainly
  rather than pointing a funding link at a slug that does not exist — the
  last thing a funding page should be is broken. Revisit only if the owner
  raises it again.
